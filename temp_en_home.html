Our Story

Le Temps d’un fût is the story of three friends, passionate about beer, lovers of microbreweries, and Quebec creativity in this field.

During the organization of various events, we realized that access to high-performance beer tap models was very difficult, and that their setup was often laborious.

Aware of the climate issues and the importance of reducing waste, we questioned how our company could be eco-friendly.
That’s why we had the idea to offer a beer distribution service in keg form and provide eco-CUPs to minimize waste resulting from traditional distribution.

Renting a tap is very advantageous for several reasons.
In addition to being very economical, it allows a greater number of people to discover the wonders of Quebec microbrewery.
It also valorizes the work of Quebec’s craft brewers by using professional-quality taps at a price accessible to everyone.
